Ticket Assistant Privacy Policy

Discord Application: Ticket Assistant (ID 1486924984730255431)
Last updated: 4 September 2026

Ticket Assistant is the official community operations bot for Tomb Busters. It is installed only on the game’s official Discord servers and is not a publicly distributed third-party bot.

1. What we collect

From a small, administrator-configured list of public lobby channels only:

  • Message text
  • Attachment URLs
  • Discord user ID and server nickname
  • Channel name, server ID/name, message ID, message link, and timestamp

We do not read direct messages, unlisted channels, or ticket-form submissions as “message content tracking.” Ticket flows use Discord Interactions (buttons and slash commands).

2. How we use it

  • Sync public lobby messages to an internal Feishu (Lark) Bitable so community staff can review and follow up.
  • Generate internal hourly and daily community-sentiment summaries (topics, mood, risk hints) for staff only.

We do not sell message content, use it for advertising, or share it with unrelated third parties.

3. Where it is stored and how long

  • Local database on our servers: hot message data is kept for about 7 days, then moved to an internal archive used only for operations review.
  • Feishu Bitable: retained as an internal operations dashboard. This copy is not automatically deleted after 30 days.

Derived staff reports (hourly/daily summaries) are also stored internally and may be pushed to internal Feishu chats.

4. AI / machine learning

Message text from those public lobbies may be sent to an Alibaba Cloud Qwen-compatible API for one-time inference (classification and summarization) in order to produce staff reports. We do not use Discord message content to train, fine-tune, or otherwise improve machine-learning models, and we do not authorize model providers to train on this data.

5. Opt-out and deletion

  • Do not post in the monitored public lobby channels if you do not want that public chat stored for operations.
  • Server administrators can remove a channel from the allowlist or remove the bot from a channel/server to stop further collection.
  • To request deletion of stored message content associated with your Discord user ID, open a support ticket with Ticket Assistant on an official Tomb Busters Discord server (Bug Report / support forum) and write “data deletion request”, including your Discord user ID.

6. Contact

Data deletion and privacy questions: open a Ticket Assistant ticket on an official Tomb Busters Discord server, or email support@giantgames.global.

Ticket Assistant 隐私政策

Discord 应用：Ticket Assistant（ID 1486924984730255431）
最近更新：2026 年 9 月 4 日

Ticket Assistant 是《Tomb Busters》官方社区运营机器人，仅安装在游戏官方 Discord 服务器，不向第三方服务器公开分发。

1. 我们收集什么

仅来自管理员预先配置的少量公开大厅频道：

  • 消息正文
  • 附件链接
  • Discord 用户 ID、服务器内昵称
  • 频道名称、服务器 ID/名称、消息 ID、消息链接、时间

我们不读取私信、未配置频道。工单流程使用 Discord 交互组件（按钮、斜杠命令），不属于大厅消息内容追踪。

2. 用途

  • 将公开大厅消息同步到内部飞书多维表，供社区运营查看和跟进。
  • 基于上述消息生成仅供内部使用的小时/每日舆情摘要（话题、情绪、风险线索）。

我们不会出售消息内容，不会用于广告，也不会提供给无关第三方。

3. 存储位置与期限

  • 我方服务器本地数据库：消息热数据约保留 7 天，之后归档到内部归档库，仅供运营排查。
  • 飞书多维表：作为内部运营看板保留，目前不会在 30 天后自动删除。

小时报/日报等衍生内容也会保存在内部，并可能推送到内部飞书群。

4. 人工智能 / 机器学习

上述公开大厅文本可能被发送至阿里云通义千问兼容 API，用于一次性推理（分类与摘要）以生成运营报告。 我们不会使用 Discord 消息内容训练、微调或改进任何机器学习模型，也不授权模型供应商用这些数据训练。

5. 退出与删除

  • 如不希望公开聊天被运营存储，请不要在被监控的公开大厅发言。
  • 服务器管理员可将频道移出监听名单，或将机器人移出频道/服务器，以停止后续采集。
  • 如需删除与您 Discord 用户 ID 相关的已存消息，请在官方 Discord 通过 Ticket Assistant 提交工单（Bug / 客服论坛），标题或内容注明「数据删除申请」，并提供您的 Discord 用户 ID。

6. 联系方式

数据删除与隐私问题：请在官方 Discord 通过 Ticket Assistant 提交工单，或发送邮件至 support@giantgames.global。
